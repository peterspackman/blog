Visualisations

Interactive explorations of physics and chemistry concepts.

Quantum

Schrödinger equation in 1D

Stationary states and time evolution in harmonic, box, double-well, Morse and lattice potentials.

Particle in a 2D box

Superpositions of stationary states in a square box, shown as evolving probability densities.

Particle in a 3D box

Probability clouds of a particle in a cubic box, rendered by volume ray marching.

Spherical harmonics and hydrogen orbitals

Separation of variables for central potentials: angular and radial parts, real and complex orbitals, and their linear combinations.

Bands from molecular orbitals

Hückel chains and rings: discrete MO levels merging into a band, with filling, doping and Peierls gaps.

Crystallography

Bragg's law

Reflections from crystal planes, path difference and how intensity peaks sharpen with more planes.

X-ray diffraction

X-ray diffraction patterns, reciprocal lattice, and electron density.

Wulff construction

Equilibrium crystal shapes from surface energies, in 2D and 3D, from cubes to needles to droplets.

Fourier transform

2D Fourier transforms with pattern drawing and wallpaper symmetry.

Simulation

Molecular dynamics

Real-time Lennard-Jones and Coulomb dynamics: argon, condensation, NaCl, mixing and ions in a field.

Grand canonical Monte Carlo

Particle insertion and deletion at fixed chemical potential: adsorption in pores, a model zeolite and on charged surfaces.
